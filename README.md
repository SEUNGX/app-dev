## White Chicks

### White Chicks is a 2004 American comedy film directed by Keenen Ivory Wayans.
---

**The story follows two FBI agents, Kevin and Marcus Copeland (played by Shawn and Marlon Wayans) who go undercover as two rich white sisters, Tiffany and Brittany Wilson, to solve a kidnapping case.**

**The brothers use disguises, makeup, and prosthetics to transform into *"white chicks*,” leading to wild comedic situations as they try to blend into high society.**

**The film gave us unforgettable scenes, like the “A Thousand Miles” car sing-along, and countless quotable lines (“Yo, hold my poodle!”).**

**Despite mixed reviews from critics, the movie was a box office hit, earning over $113 million worldwide. It later became a cult favorite, especially for its outrageous humor and meme-worthy moments.**
